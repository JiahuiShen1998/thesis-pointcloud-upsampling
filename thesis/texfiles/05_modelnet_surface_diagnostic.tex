\subsection{Secondary Dense-Reference Surface Diagnostic}
\label{sec:geometry-audit}
The archived dense-reference experiment additionally measured point-to-face
distance after independently centring and normalising each cloud and mesh.
Its nonzero original-input baseline reflects that coordinate convention.
These values do not estimate absolute distance to the original surface
in a common frame. Table~\ref{tab:p2f-audit} retains every recorded mean
P2F row, including the original and sparse controls.

A low value in this table therefore answers a different question from
equal-cardinality CD and HD. Recentring and rescaling can contribute to
the residual even when points were sampled from the underlying mesh.
The P2F table is preserved as a completed diagnostic, while the
equal-cardinality distances remain the main geometric comparison.
Full per-object values and paired dense-reference summaries remain in
the accompanying evidence package.
